The recording

Creation notes

A song of voices, whistles, one synthesizer, and a nylon-string guitar. I love imitating opera singers from time to time, as it is one of the truest and most difficult, perhaps purest, of art forms.

Sing it around a campfire, in a field, or at the edge of the ocean… and if you listen, it will sing to you… cue your best “fa la la.”
